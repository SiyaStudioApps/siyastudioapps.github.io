Color by Number: Color Studio

Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026. This policy covers the Android game com.colorstudio.colorbynumber, provided by SIYA Studio.

Local coloring data

Progress, favorites, settings, replay history, hints and picture unlocks are stored on your device. The game has no user accounts or SIYA Studio server for uploading these records. Android backup and restore may apply according to your device settings.

Advertisements

The game integrates Google Mobile Ads for banners, interstitials and optional rewarded videos. The current internal test build uses Google test ad units. Ad requests communicate with Google. The SDK may process IP address (including approximate location), device and app information, identifiers where available, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Current requests are tagged for child-directed treatment and under age of consent, with a general-audience content limit. This does not mean that no technical data is transmitted.

Read Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Information.

Saving and sharing

Saving writes a picture to your device. Sharing happens only when you choose a destination through Android; that destination handles the image under its own policy. The game has no public gallery or chat. Older Android versions may require storage permission to save images.

Children and families

The coloring game is intended for a broad audience including children. It does not ask for names, email addresses, precise location or account credentials. Parents can manage device permissions and local data through Android settings. Rewarded videos require a user action.

Retention and deletion

Progress and settings remain until app storage is cleared or the app is removed, subject to Android backup. Clearing storage removes progress and unlocks. Exported pictures remain until you delete them from your photo storage. Google controls retention of information it processes under its own policies; SIYA Studio cannot directly erase Google records.

Support

Contact ibnyaichsaad@gmail.com for support and privacy requests. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you provide, use it to respond, and retain it as needed for the request. Do not send passwords or children's personal information.

Security and updates

We rely on platform storage and standard network protections. No system guarantees absolute security. This page will be updated when data practices change; check the effective date above.
